Support Bot — Handle customer queries 24/7

Answers inbound questions from your Knowledge Base around the clock. Escalates to your team only when it genuinely needs a human decision. Reduces support load by 60–80% on day one.

Bot answers static timing and availability from your KB — "Dr. Sharma is available Mon/Wed/Fri", "OPD closes at 8 PM". For booking, it collects the patient's name, preferred date, and phone number, then your front desk team confirms the appointment.

Bot answers general post-procedure care questions from your KB — not the patient's personal records. For anything patient-specific, it collects the patient's name and concern and escalates to your nursing or front-desk team.
Sample conversation

2Pre-Appointment Reminders & Instructions›
Bot sends a reminder 24 hours before every appointment, including any preparation instructions (fasting, documents to bring). Dramatically reduces no-show rates and the clinic's follow-up call workload.
Reminder sent by bot
📅 Hi Meena, your appointment with Dr. Patel is tomorrow at 10 AM. Please bring your previous X-rays or MRI reports if available. The clinic is at [address]. See you tomorrow! 🩺
3Follow-Up Appointment Booking›
After a visit, bot proactively messages the patient to book the follow-up the doctor recommended — "come back in 2 weeks" becomes an actual confirmed booking, not a good intention that gets forgotten.
Message sent after consultation
📅 Hi Meena, Dr. Patel recommended a follow-up in 2 weeks to review your X-rays. Book now? Available: Mon 11 AM, Wed 10 AM, Fri 3 PM. 📅
