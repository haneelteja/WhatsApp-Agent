02 Product & Market Understanding

Product-Market Fit Hypotheses

  1. D2C brands handling 100+ daily WhatsApp queries currently respond manually or use basic auto-reply — they will pay ₹8,000+/month to automate 80% of responses with measurable CSAT improvement. Needs validation
  2. Healthcare clinics lose 30%+ of appointment bookings to missed WhatsApp messages after hours — the Appointment Bot directly recovers revenue. Needs validation
  3. The 4-bot architecture (Support + Sales + Lifecycle + Appointment) justifies a 2–3× price premium over single-bot competitors because it replaces 3 separate tools or 1–2 FTEs. Needs validation
  4. Restaurants, salons, and fitness studios losing 25–40% of WhatsApp booking requests to missed or delayed replies will pay ₹5,999/month for an Appointment Bot that handles booking 24/7. Needs validation
  5. DPDPA compliance features are a differentiator for regulated verticals (BFSI, healthcare) who cannot use non-compliant platforms after May 2027. High confidence

Limitations to Acknowledge

  • Dependent on Meta's WhatsApp Business API policies — pricing, template approvals, and tier limits are outside Alphabot's control.
  • LLM costs (Claude API) scale with usage — margin compression at high message volumes must be modelled.
  • No native CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) integrations yet — integration complexity is a mid-market barrier.
  • Voice capability requires separate onboarding (Exotel + Sarvam credentials) — increases time-to-value for voice use cases.
  • Appointment Bot requires booking system integration for full slot management — standalone version handles inquiry routing only.

Regulatory Considerations

DPDPA 2023 — Full enforcement May 2027. AI platforms processing personal data via WhatsApp conversations must implement: explicit opt-in consent, data principal rights (access/erasure), Data Processing Agreements with clients, and a documented grievance mechanism. Penalties up to ₹250 crore per violation. Alphabot must build DPDPA-compliant consent flows and offer DPA templates to enterprise clients now, as it becomes a hard procurement requirement by 2026.

  • Meta Compliance: Template pre-approval, opt-in documentation, quality ratings, messaging tier limits (1K → 10K → 100K/day). These are BSP requirements all customers must satisfy.
  • TRAI / DoT: WhatsApp is OTT; DLT registration (SMS rules) does NOT apply. Current DoT stance is non-interventionist on OTT messaging platforms.
  • RBI Guidelines (BFSI): Conversational AI used in lending/insurance must not constitute regulated financial advice. Requires guardrails and disclaimer language.

15 Sales Process & Qualification

Recommended Framework: SPICED

SPICED (Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, Decision) fits Alphabot's SMB/mid-market motion better than MEDDICC (too enterprise-heavy) or BANT (too transactional).

LetterQuestion TypeDiscovery Questions for Alphabot
S — SituationUnderstand current state"How many WhatsApp messages does your team receive per day?" / "How many people manage your WhatsApp right now?" / "Which tools do you use today for customer communication?"
P — PainSurface the real problem"What happens to messages that come in after 6 PM?" / "How long does it typically take your team to respond?" / "How many booking requests do you miss or respond to late?"
I — ImpactQuantify the pain"How much time per day does your team spend on repetitive WhatsApp queries?" / "What would recovering 5 missed bookings per week mean for your revenue?" / "If you recovered 20% more leads from WhatsApp, what would that mean for monthly revenue?"
C — Critical EventFind the forcing function"Is there a product launch, festive season, or expansion planned where volume will spike?" / "Is your team stretched heading into Q4?" / "Are you opening a new outlet or location soon?"
E — DecisionMap the buying process"If we build a trial for your WhatsApp number this week, who else needs to see it before a decision?" / "Is budget something you control, or does it require approval?" / "What would a successful first 30 days look like for you?"
